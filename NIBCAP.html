About the unit

Building the next generation of industry-ready professionals.

The NIBCAP Career Acceleration Unit is part of the Resource Portal & Job Desk (RPJD), an initiative of the Nigerian Society of Engineers, Ikeja Branch. It supports engineers and built-environment professionals through structured learning, practical development and career acceleration activities.

NIBCAP focuses on equipping participants with relevant capabilities that strengthen professional effectiveness, adaptability and contribution to industry.

Our mandate

Skills that translate into impact.

Design and promote career development initiatives that connect professional knowledge with practical application, continuous learning and workplace performance.
